title: Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some
bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in sugars.

The overall classroom equation is:

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
(in the presence of light and chlorophyll)

A-Level depth separates two stages:
- Light-dependent reactions in the thylakoid membranes produce ATP and reduced NADP, and split water (photolysis) releasing oxygen.
- Light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle) in the stroma use ATP and reduced NADP to reduce carbon dioxide to carbohydrate.

Limiting factors at this level: light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration,
and temperature. The rate is limited by the factor in shortest supply.

Common misconception: plants only photosynthesise and do not respire.
They respire continuously; net gas exchange depends on light.
