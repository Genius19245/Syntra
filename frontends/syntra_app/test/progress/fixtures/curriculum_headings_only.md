# Curriculum Plan

## Learner Profile
- Level: GCSE
- Subject: Chemistry
- Topic: Ionic bonding

## Curriculum Structure

### 1. Charged particles in atoms
- Purpose: Recall protons, neutrons, and electrons

### 2. How ions form
- Purpose: Show electron transfer

### 3. Giant ionic lattices
- Purpose: Connect structure to properties

## Teaching Sequence
1. Recap atomic structure
2. Build a sodium chloride example
