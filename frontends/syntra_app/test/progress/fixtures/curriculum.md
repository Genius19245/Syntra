# Curriculum Plan

## Learner Profile
- Level: A-Level
- Subject: Physics
- Topic: Electromagnetic induction
- Assumed Prior Knowledge: Current and voltage
- Learning Goal: Prepare for an exam

## Prerequisites
Copy from the Prerequisite Agent analysis.

### Missing
- Magnetic flux and flux density
- Right-hand grip rule for field direction

## Learning Objectives
Copy from the Learning Objectives Agent.

1. Explain how a changing magnetic flux produces an induced electromotive force.
2. Calculate the induced EMF for a given rate of change of flux.
3. Apply Lenz's law to predict the direction of an induced current.

## Curriculum Structure

### 1. Magnetic flux
- Purpose: Define flux before induction
- Concepts: Flux, flux density
- Required depth: A-Level definition

### 2. Faraday's law
- Purpose: Link changing flux to induced EMF
- Concepts: Faraday's law
- Required depth: Quantitative

### 3. Lenz's law
- Purpose: Predict current direction
- Concepts: Opposition, energy
- Required depth: Qualitative plus examples

## Teaching Sequence
1. Recap current and voltage
2. Define flux
3. Derive Faraday's law
4. Apply Lenz's law
5. Worked exam item

## Difficulty / Depth
Kept at A-Level algebraic demand.

## Expected Learning Outcome
Learners can explain and calculate induced EMF.
