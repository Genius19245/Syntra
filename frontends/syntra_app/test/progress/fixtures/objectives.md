# Learning Objectives

## Target
Subject: Physics
Topic: Electromagnetic induction
Education Level: A-Level

## Objectives

By the end of the learning experience, the learner will be able to:

1. Explain how a changing magnetic flux produces an induced electromotive force.
2. Calculate the induced EMF for a given rate of change of flux.
3. Apply Lenz's law to predict the direction of an induced current.
4. Compare Faraday's law statements used in A-Level exam questions.

## Objective Types

- Understanding
- Application
- Application
- Analysis

## Progression

Objectives move from explaining the mechanism to calculation, then to direction and comparison.
